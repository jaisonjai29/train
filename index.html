When does booking open?

Pick your journey date. We do the rest.
Advance booking opens 60 days before the journey date (journey day excluded) at 8:00 AM IST. On opening day only Aadhaar-verified IRCTC accounts can book online, and Tatkal needs an Aadhaar OTP.

Alarm & style

Make it ring loud. Make it yours.
Alarms ring (sound, vibration, full screen) while RailPing is open. Browsers cannot ring a closed page, so also tap “+ Calendar” on a trip for background reminders.
General booking opens 60 days before the journey (journey day excluded) at 8:00 AM. Tatkal opens 1 day before: 10:00 AM for AC, 11:00 AM for non-AC. All times IST. Confirm on IRCTC; special trains can differ.
